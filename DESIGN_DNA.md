# Design DNA: B&B Home Improvements concept

**Archetype:** a craftsman's job log, led by type. B&B has no photos online that we can use, so the site is built from type, color and real job lines from reviews instead of stock photos.

**Signature:** the tape measure. A yellow blade with inch ticks runs under the hero, under each page head and on top of the footer. It pulls out from the left when it scrolls into view. The kicker mark is a short run of ruler ticks. Inch 12 is red.

**Motion:** "measure." The tape pulls out (scaleX from 0.04 to 1, 1.3s). Sections rise 18px and fade in. Reduced motion turns it all off.

**Type:** Rokkitt (slab serif, 500 to 800) for display. It reads like a stamp on lumber. Karla for body text. Neither font has been used in another LuminArch demo.

**Color:** sampled from the B&B logo (red and blue), plus tape yellow and plywood.
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| paper | #f6efe1 | page |
| ply | #ead9b8 | tiles |
| red | #a3201c | brand, CTAs, numbers |
| denim | #1f3a68 | button shadow, clipboard, footer |
| tape | #f2c230 | signature blade, placeholder tags |
| ink | #1c1a17 | text, rooms band |
| mute | #5b5348 | secondary text |

**Section shapes, home:** a split hero with a tilted clipboard of recent jobs; a giant outlined "6th" next to the mailbox review; a dark bento of rooms (bathrooms get the big red tile because they are the most common job in the reviews); three reviews with a denim lead card; a five step ledger where every step is a review quote; and a denim footer sign reading "Call Bobby."

**Buttons:** red with a hard denim offset shadow, like a sign painted on plywood.

**Placeholders:** yellow "Placeholder" tags for project photos, Bobby's story, licensing and insurance, and a live review feed.
