## 2. Where B&B shows up today

- Google Business Profile: name, phone, Zephyr Rd (no street number), hours Mon to Sat 7 to 7, one owner post with a kitchen photo, a photo of the hand painted sign as the cover. No website link.
- Third party aggregators (ContractorsRanked, BuildZoom style sites) that B&B does not control. One lists a different street address (8568 Tissington St), which may confuse people.
- Name collisions: "B & B Home Improvement & Cabinets" and other NC B&Bs. A search for "B&B Home Improvements Fayetteville" is noisy.

## 4. The opening

B&B has no site, so anyone who hears "call Bobby" and searches him lands on a Google card and a stack of aggregators. The reviews are the asset: 58 at 4.9, and they are specific (a lamp post mailbox after five other handymen failed, a rim joist in days, three bathrooms in one house). The demo turns them into the site: a job log of real dated jobs, a process told only in customer quotes, and a room by room view where bathrooms lead because they dominate the reviews.

## 5. Pitch points with numbers

1. 0 websites today. The Google card says "Add website".
2. 58 Google reviews at 4.9, and the 8 we can read in full are all 5 stars. 4 of the 8 are bathroom remodels.
3. Concept loads in 2.6s LCP at 0.10 MB. The local competitors measured today take 7.6 to 12.5s.
4. A page with one phone number, one address area and one name would cut through the B&B name collisions and the wrong Tissington St address.

## 6. Risks and honest caveats

- **License.** No NC GC license found. Jobs under $40,000 don't need one, but a "licensed and insured" line is the first thing remodeling sites show. The demo makes no license or insurance claim. Ask Bobby before pitching hard.
- **Registration.** No entity record found on OpenCorporates; the NC SOS search could not be checked. Possibly a sole proprietor.
- **No photos.** Every photo spot is a labeled placeholder. Bobby would need to send job photos.
- **30+ years** is a customer's estimate, quoted as such.
- He may not want a site. He gets work from referrals and Google already.
